The Problem

Graph of Black Lung Disease cases in the past decade

Caveminers are prone to long-term diseases due to long-term exposure of poor air quality, which shreds lung lining. In recent years, the rate of black lung disease, which is a serious health concern that affects miners and other workers exposed to coal dust over extended periods, has increased. This increase is mainly caused by a lack of safety precautions before entering a cavesite.

Existing Solutions

An Autonomous Driving Robot used in a 2021 Study

Research has explored the use of robotic systems for underground inspection and environmental monitoring. Autonomous robots can carry sensors into areas that may be difficult for people to inspect, while obstacle-detection systems help robots navigate around surrounding objects. Hexapod robots, which use six legs instead of wheels, are also studied for their potential to move across uneven terrain.
